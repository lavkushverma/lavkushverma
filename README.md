### 🚀 Professional Profile

I am a dedicated **Cloud & DevOps Engineer** based in **Mumbai, India**, specializing in designing, automating, and managing scalable, secure, and resilient cloud infrastructure on **AWS**. My expertise lies in bridging the gap between development and operations through **Infrastructure as Code (Terraform)**, **Container Orchestration (EKS/ECS)**, and robust **CI/CD** pipelines.

I focus on building production-ready environments with a strong emphasis on **AWS Security**, **Cloud Networking**, and **Observability**, ensuring high availability and cost-optimization for enterprise-grade workloads.
